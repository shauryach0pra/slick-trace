# Appendix B — Licence and data-rights

Data provenance is checked by evaluators, and a single line claiming "open data"
is weaker than the terms themselves. This appendix records what is actually
shipped, under what terms, and what would have to be recorded before any real
source is added.

**Current status, stated plainly: no third-party data is included in this
repository, and none has been retrieved.** Every dataset here is generated by
this codebase. That makes this appendix short today, and it is the honest
version rather than a placeholder.

independently confirmed label.

**The fictional MMSIs are worth stating explicitly.** They are drawn from a real
Maritime Identification Digit range but are randomly generated and correspond to
no actual vessel. No inference about any real ship should be drawn from any
identifier appearing in this repository, in the interface, or in any screenshot
or video frame produced from it.

## What is deliberately *not* here

No Zenodo Sentinel-1 imagery, no Danish Maritime Authority or NOAA AIS, no
Copernicus ERA5 or Marine reanalysis. The build environment has no network route
to any of them, so nothing was downloaded and no terms were accepted.

This matters for how the results are read: **synthetic ranking accuracy is not a
measurement of real-world attribution accuracy**, and the validation report says
so above its first table.

---

## What must be recorded before any real source is added

Nothing in this appendix transfers to real data. Each source needs its own entry
capturing:

- **Source and version** — publisher, dataset title, DOI or permanent
  identifier, part or version, retrieval date.
- **Licence identifier** — the exact licence as published, captured as retrieved
  text with its URL, not paraphrased.
- **Redistribution status** — whether the data may be bundled inside a container
  image or must be fetched by the recipient. This determines what the incident
  capsule may ship.
- **Attribution string** — the exact wording required, reproduced in the README,
  the report export and the interface's data panel. Not only in a slide.
- **Derivative works** — whether trained weights, cleaned tracks or derived
  products inherit conditions from the source.
- **Commercial and operational use** — whether the terms permit the deployment
  being described. Required before any zero-cost agency-deployment claim.
- **Access constraints** — account, token or scope needed; rate limits; and
  whether raw historical tracks are actually included or only derived products.

Sources that will each need an entry: Zenodo Sentinel-1 oil-spill dataset Parts
I–III (including any Sentinel-1 upstream attribution the collection carries);
DMA historical AIS; NOAA MarineCadastre AIS, recorded separately because its
terms differ; Global Fishing Watch, recording explicitly that its public products
do not guarantee arbitrary raw historical tracks; and Copernicus ERA5 and Marine
reanalysis with the required Copernicus attribution statement.

## Consequences to check before the freeze

- **If a source forbids redistribution**, the incident capsule ships a fetch
  script and a manifest rather than the data, and the offline demonstration uses
  the synthetic case. The current build already works this way by necessity, so
  no change would be needed — but the reason must be stated as a licence
  decision rather than left looking like an accident of the sandbox.
- **Required attribution strings** appear in the README, the report export and
  the interface, not only in the presentation.
- **No zero-licensing-cost deployment claim** is made unless the recorded terms
  support it. No such claim currently appears anywhere in this repository.

## Candidate dependencies, evaluated and not adopted

Four upstream tools were assessed for the real-data path. None is used today —
the shipped system depends only on the packages in `requirements.lock` — but the
licence position of each was checked before the freeze, because adopting the
obvious one would change this repository's licence.

| Tool | What it would do here | Licence position | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| OpenDrift / OpenOil | Replace the in-repo transport model with an established Lagrangian oil drift and weathering engine | **GPL v2** | Linking it would require relicensing this repository from MIT to GPL. A deliberate decision, not a detail — deferred until the real-data path is actually built |
| NOAA ADIOS (`adios_db`) | Real oil properties — density, evaporation, emulsification — instead of varying them blind | Openly available, unencumbered | Safe to adopt. The most attractive of the four, and the one that directly serves assumption A-oil-properties |
| GSHHG shorelines | Higher-resolution coastline than the Natural Earth 10 m the cases use | LGPL since 2.2.2 (source data itself public domain) | Usable; conditions attach to the library rather than the data |
| WDPA via `pywdpa` | Real marine protected areas for the exposure forecast | Open, with a **mandatory citation string** | Adoptable, but the attribution must then appear in the README, the report export and the interface — not only in a slide |

Recorded here rather than discovered later: a reviewer asking "why not just use
OpenDrift?" should find that the question was considered and answered, not that
it was missed.
