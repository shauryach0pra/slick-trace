# Calibration report

60 cases generated separately from the benchmark, 236 (score, outcome) pairs across all candidates including losers.

| | Brier | ECE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raw ensemble win fraction | 0.0118 | 0.0395 |
| After isotonic calibration | 0.0169 | 0.0169 |

> Fitted on 118 pairs, evaluated on 118 held out. A map evaluated on its own fitting data would report an improvement it has not earned.

> **What this establishes.** Calibration of the ensemble win fraction against synthetic truth, fitted on cases generated by this repository and evaluated on a held-out half. It establishes that the score is well-behaved on cases from this generator. It is not evidence of calibration on real imagery, real AIS or real forcing.

> **Reading the two metrics together.** Isotonic calibration reduces ECE and raises Brier here. That is not a failure of the fit: the raw score is already close to calibrated, so the map has little to correct, and what it learns is to sharpen the near-extremes to 0 and 1. Sharpening lowers the average gap between stated confidence and observed rate while paying a larger penalty on the occasional miss. The finding is that **the ensemble win fraction was already well-behaved on this generator**, not that calibration rescued it.

> **Effect on assumption A11.** A11 declined to call the win fraction a probability because no calibration against held-out outcomes existed. One now does, and the caution was warranted but conservative: measured ECE is 0.040 over 118 held-out pairs. The score remains an evidence score rather than a probability of responsibility — calibration against synthetic truth measures the generator, not the ocean.

> **Why a separate set.** The eight benchmark cases cannot support this: their fragility grids record only the leader per perturbation, and 80 perturbations of one scenario are not 80 independent observations. This set is generated separately, with seeds disjoint from CASE_PLAN, and contributes to no headline number.
